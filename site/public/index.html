The art

More than 1,300 species, colored by hand.

In the nineteenth century, a handful of naturalists set out to paint every bird they could find, and to print each picture by hand. John James Audubon’s The Birds of America and John Gould’s books of Europe, Australia, Asia and Great Britain are among the most beautiful books ever made. In 2010, a single copy of The Birds of America sold for $11.5 million.

Featherframe shows their illustrations one at a time, with the species’ name, its Latin name and the legend engraved beneath it.

How it works

Meet the birds you only hear.

Most birds nearby are heard long before they are seen. Featherframe puts a name and a portrait to each call, so over a season you come to know who lives around you.

BirdWeather is a public map of listening stations in gardens and parks around the world. Each one uses BirdNET, software from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, to name the birds it hears. Choose a station near you, or near somewhere you love: a parent’s garden, or the coast you visit every summer. When it names a species, the frame shows it. You can also add a station of your own.

The collage

Each night, the day’s menagerie.

The frame shows every species heard that day on one sheet, numbered and keyed like a page in an old natural history book.

If you add an OpenAI key, Featherframe paints the day’s species together in one scene. Without it, the day is laid out in the original illustrations. AI is off unless you turn it on, and everything it paints carries a ✦.

✦ Each collage was painted by AI from the species heard that day at one listening station.

Questions
Do I need Wi-Fi?
Yes. The frame joins your Wi-Fi to fetch each new illustration, and needs nothing else after setup.
What if a species near me was never illustrated?
The frame shows its name, set in the type of the old books. If you add an OpenAI key, it can paint an illustration in their style instead, marked ✦.
What is hosting, and what does it cost?
Hosting picks each illustration and sends it to your frame. It is included with every frame, and there is no subscription.
Is my data private?
The frame has no camera and no microphone. Your account holds your frame’s settings and the species it has shown, and nothing is sold or shared.
